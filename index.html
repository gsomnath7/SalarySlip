About Teacher Salary Portal

The Teacher Salary Portal is a dedicated digital platform developed to empower educational staff and teachers with instant access to their monthly payroll summaries.

Teachers can review earnings, voluntary deductions, bank credits, and generate high-resolution print copies or portable PDF files with a single click.

Key Features

Strict Security Secure authentication based on individual Employee ID and credentials.
Archived Payroll Records Select any past month and academic year to access historical slips.
Verification QR Code Generated slips feature authentic verification QR codes for validation.
PDF Export Ready Save clean, vectorized PDF records directly to your phone or computer.
